Before constructing this gravitational theory, we provide supplementary calculations based on existing literature and justify the validity of the gravitational theory by comparison with the real-number case, for instance, the curvatures of de Sitter(dS), anti-de Sitter(AdS), and Euclidean space should be positive, negative, and zero, respectively. Finally, as an example on the gravity side of the $p$-adic AdS$_2$/CFT$_1$, we present the action for a scalar field coupled to gravity.
