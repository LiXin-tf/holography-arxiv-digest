中文摘要：着眼于理解平直时空全息，我们研究规范 N=2 超引力中渐近 AdS4 黑洞的平直时空极限。利用全息重整化，我们仔细分析该背景下各种黑洞 on-shell 作用量的平直时空极限，并明确在此极限下贡献配分函数的状态。我们发现若干特征：大黑洞导致配分函数中的发散，可理解为热力学体积发散。同时，在半经典层面，小黑洞重现其平直时空对应物的配分函数，并且我们阐明了带电荷和转动解在 BPS、(近)极值或非极值黑洞两侧之间的映射。最后，通过研究 M 理论中 BPS Kerr-Newman-AdS4 黑洞的合适平直极限，我们得到平直时空中的带电 BPS 解。在对偶侧，我们确定了已知的 S^1×S^2 上大 N ABJM 配分函数的类 Carroll 极限，并证明它重现渐近平直时空中 BPS 带电黑洞的正确熵。

English: With a view towards understanding flat space holography, we study the flat space limit of asymptotically $AdS_4$ black holes in gauged $\mathcal{N}=2$ supergravity. Using holographic renormalisation, we carefully analyse the flat space limit of the on-shell action of various black holes in this setting and make explicit the states that contribute to the partition function in this limit. We find several features: large black holes lead to a divergence in the partition function which can be understood as a thermodynamic volume divergence. Meanwhile, semiclassically, small black holes reproduce the partition function of their flat space counterparts, and we clarify the mapping between BPS, (near-)extremal or non-extremal black holes on both sides for charged and rotating solutions. Finally, by studying a suitable flat limit of the BPS Kerr-Newman-$AdS_4$ black hole in M-theory, we obtain a charged BPS solution in flat space. On the dual side, we determine a Carrollian-like limit on the known ABJM partition function on $S^1\times S^2$ at large $N$, and show that it reproduces the correct entropy of the BPS charged black hole in asymptotically flat space.
